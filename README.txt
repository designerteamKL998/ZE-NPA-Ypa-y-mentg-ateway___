Combined Fund Transfer + Bank Login Demo

Start at index.html (Fund Transfer bank selection).
Select Maybank or Public Bank, then click Proceed to Bank to open the matching local login page.
Other bank options remain visible but their login pages are not included in this bundle.

Folders:
- index.html, style.css, script.js: Fund Transfer bank selection page
- banks/maybank/: Maybank yellow/black login page
- banks/public-bank/: Public Bank red login page
- assets/: Fund Transfer assets

Only assets without references in the related HTML/CSS/JS were removed. Existing desktop/mobile layouts and original visual assets are retained.

Prototype note: bank login forms are local demo forms only; no credentials are transmitted or processed by a server.
